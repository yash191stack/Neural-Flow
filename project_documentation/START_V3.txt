🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Port 3001 already in use
Solution: taskkill /F /IM node.exe

Problem: Frontend won't connect
Solution: Check backend is running at http://localhost:3001

Problem: No events showing
Solution: Backend sends event_history on connect - wait 2 seconds

Problem: AI not responding
Solution: Switch to AI mode in Dashboard, wait for model training
